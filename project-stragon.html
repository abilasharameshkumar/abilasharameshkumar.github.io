Project Overview

My team and I designed the "Stragon," a wagon-stroller hybrid that addressed the usability limitations of traditional child transport systems through a structured, human-centred design process.

The project was driven by the need to reduce user exclusion - designing a product that works for a wider range of caregivers and children, including those with mobility challenges. Every design decision was grounded in human factors data and validated using the Cambridge Exclusion Calculator (CEC).

I contributed significantly to the CAD modelling, engineering drawings, and human factors calculations throughout all three design iterations. The final design reduced the user exclusion rate from 15.5% to 11.8%.

Key Design Features
  • Dual terrain system (hand and foot braking) for safety on varied terrains
  • Removable, rotatable child seats with harness systems for flexibility and security
  • Mobility walker-style handlebar for improved ergonomics and accessibility
  • Shock-absorbing suspension and hybrid wheel system (track and caster) for stability
  • Integrated storage and accessibility ramp for user convenience
Design Process

The design followed a structured, three-iteration process. Each iteration began with concept generation, moved into evaluation using human factors criteria and CEC analysis, and fed directly into the next round of refinement. No design decision was made without first validating its impact on usability and inclusivity.

Iteration 1 - Concept Exploration

The first iteration focused on establishing the core form factor. Multiple wagon and stroller configurations were sketched and evaluated against user requirements. CEC analysis was run on each concept to identify which features most significantly impacted exclusion rates.

Iteration 2 - Refinement

The strongest features from Iteration 1 were carried forward and integrated into a more developed concept. The handlebar system, seat configuration, and wheel layout were refined based on feedback and updated CEC scoring. CAD development began at this stage.

Iteration 3 - Integration and Finalisation

The third iteration consolidated all subsystems into a single cohesive design. The suspension, storage, and accessibility ramp were added. Final CEC analysis confirmed the reduction in user exclusion from 15.5% to 11.8%, validating the design decisions made across all three rounds.

Final Engineering Drawings

The final design was fully detailed in SolidWorks, producing complete engineering drawings with dimensions, tolerances, and assembly specifications. These drawings translate the design intent into a manufacturable, well-documented product.
